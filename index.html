Research

Smart Drop

An IoT-based solution for secure and efficient package handling. Ashmeet Kaur and Juhi Sharma, B.Tech Computer Science Engineering, Jaypee Institute of Information Technology, Noida. Supervised by Dr. Prakash Kumar.

Package theft, missed deliveries and poor delivery updates are a growing cost for e-commerce customers and logistics companies. Smart Drop is a locked delivery box that only opens for the right person. A user books a box and receives a one-time password. The delivery partner logs in with a six-digit employee ID, takes the order, and uses an OTP to open the box.

The hardware uses a Raspberry Pi 3 B+ and an Arduino Uno that talk over serial. A relay switches a 12 V electromagnetic lock, and a 3.5-inch LCD shows the box state. The software is a Flutter app backed by Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, with separate flows for users and delivery partners.

Twelve planned test cases passed. They cover registration, login, invalid-login handling, order creation, OTP generation, partner onboarding, order handling, the 5 V power supply, the EM lock's lock and unlock cycle, and Arduino to Raspberry Pi communication.

The report is candid about what is not finished. The current version sends no notifications by email, SMS or push. The EM lock needs continuous power to stay locked, so a backup supply is needed during outages. Future work includes delivery notifications, offline OTP handling, battery backup or solar power, end-to-end encryption, and multi-language support.
